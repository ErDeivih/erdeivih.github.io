I am working on several related applications for groceries and food. The aim is to connect deciding what to eat, identifying the ingredients needed and comparing where to buy them.

## Starting point

The grocery project collects supermarket products and prices through scraping. It supports product comparisons, grocery lists and favourites.

Recipe and calorie tracking applications are being developed as related pieces. The combined application is not finished.

## What I want to connect

Use the recipes you regularly cook and your weekly meals to prepare an ingredient list with quantities, then use supermarket comparisons to plan the purchase.

Planning around calorie goals is still future work. Preferences, quantity calculations and the tool's recommendation limits need to be defined.

## Before public access

- Review product matching, pack sizes and unit prices.
- Show price update dates and missing data.
- Connect recipes, ingredients, favourites and lists.
- Prepare a version that can be explored without real personal data.

## Status

In development, with no public demo yet. This page will collect results as individual parts become ready to show.
