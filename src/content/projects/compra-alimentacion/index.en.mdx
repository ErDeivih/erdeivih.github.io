This project brings together tools I am developing for groceries and food. I want to connect price comparison with home recipes and weekly meal planning.

## Compare and make a list

The grocery application collects supermarket products and prices through scraping. It supports comparisons, grocery lists and favourite products.

Matching equivalent products is an important part of the work: pack size and unit price matter as much as the price on the label.

## Recipes and meal tracking

Recipe and calorie tracking applications are in development. The next step is to connect them to ingredients and products in the comparison tool.

## Weekly groceries

The idea is to turn your regular meals into weekly ingredients and quantities, then use available prices to organise the purchase.

Preferences and calorie goals still need to be connected; the project is not yet an integrated planner.

## Availability

The combined project is in development and has no public version yet. I am connecting its parts and working on showing price update dates and availability.
