OpoFuente is a study tool for civil service exams. It brings documents, notes and questions together so you can move from reading a topic to practising it and reviewing mistakes.

## Organise notes

Import material into a library. Search retrieves information from documents and keeps source references so you can return to the text you are studying.

## Practise and review

The question bank supports practice sessions and reviewing errors. Review information can also be exported.

Available material and formats depend on the exam profile. The project includes work on TAI and Guardia Civil, with coverage being reviewed separately.

## Current work

I am improving note reading, revision organisation and exam formats. Current question sessions are not yet a complete official exam simulator.

A sample with publicly shareable material still needs to be prepared.

## Availability

The application is in development and does not yet have a public access link.
