OpoFuente collects civil service exam study material: notes, documents and questions. I am using it to organise studying and practise without searching through separate folders.

## Available now

The local version imports and organises material, offers a question bank and practice sessions, and supports reviewing mistakes. Search retrieves information from stored material and keeps references to its origin.

Project documentation includes TAI and Guardia Civil profiles. Coverage will be specified for each exam; imported material does not imply a complete syllabus.

## Next steps

- Improve reading and revision workflows.
- Review question coverage and sources.
- Complete exam formats that are still missing.
- Prepare a public sample with shareable material.

## Status

In local development. Question sessions are not yet a complete official exam simulator. There is no public demo linked here yet.
