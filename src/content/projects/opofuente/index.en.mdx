OpoFuente is a C1 IT civil-service syllabus with its practice. It is not a course or a generic app: it
is the common 40-topic programme, with a coverage map and questions linked to their official source.

## The problem

Preparing a C1 IT civil-service exam means studying a common 40-topic syllabus spread across law,
systems, networks and security, with different calls that share almost the whole programme. The
candidate does not know what is missing, studying is not practising and the official information
sits outside the notes.

## What it does

- **Home page** with the 12 C1 calls served by the common syllabus, plus the TAI profile.
- **Coverage map** of the 40 topics with two marks per topic: notes (complete, summary only or
  pending) and practice (with practice or pending).
- **Index** with progress by block, search by code or title and filters by notes and practice.
- **Practice** with instant marking, a question page and a link to the consolidated BOE text.
- **Visitor progress** stored in the browser, without an account.

## Data and data model

- **Model:** a curriculum of 40 topics in 5 blocks. Each topic declares notes and practice, and the
  coverage state is **calculated from the content**, never typed by hand.
- **Current content:** 17 topics with complete notes, 17 practice sets of 10 questions (170 questions)
  and 23 topics explicitly flagged as pending.
- **Calls:** 12 C1 calls in JSON, with their state (to be confirmed or closed) and an official BOE
  link.
- **Traceability:** each topic has its official source; each question has a reasoned answer and, when
  it cites an article, a link to the consolidated text.
- **Rights:** a recorded decision not to publish academy or third-party material; it stays as
  internal reference only.

## What I learned building it

- **KPIs and metrics:** coverage per topic and block calculated from the content: 17 of 40 topics, 23
  pending and 0 "summary only" topics.
- **Data quality and debugging:** state derived from the content, with no filler rows, and checked
  official links.
- **Requirements and scope:** a documented common syllabus (decisions D-005 and D-021) instead of an
  unbounded app.
- **Reports and dashboards:** a coverage map with two marks per topic and progress by block.
- **Information traceability:** every topic with a source and every question with an answer and a link
  to the official text.

## Real state

**Done and verifiable:** the static demo is published and works end to end, passing 20 of 20 Playwright
checks against the public URL (home, calls, 40 topics, coverage, marks, search, filters, marking with
a BOE link and browser persistence). 517 backend unit tests and 225 frontend unit tests, with type and
style checks green.

**Pending:** it is a static demo, with no backend, accounts or sync: progress lives in the browser.
23 topics remain, real practice sessions are not measured yet and the full app with a backend has no
public URL. The recovered TAI syllabus and the supporting PDFs are private internal reference and are
not published because of third-party rights.
