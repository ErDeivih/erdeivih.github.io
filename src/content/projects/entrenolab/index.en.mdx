EntrenoLab brings the club, its teams, sports management and session preparation into an app that
works on a phone and a tablet. The club's internal app is documented on another page (CDMPLab); this
demo is the commercial product.

## The problem

Amateur youth-football coaches run the club on spreadsheets, notes and WhatsApp groups. There is no
single source of truth: a player's minutes live in one sheet, fines in another and sessions in a
notebook, and none of it can be checked from the pitch.

## What it does

- **Real multi-user access:** sign-up and login, a sample club and per-coach isolation verified
  against the backend.
- **Sports management:** squad, matches with minutes, goals and cards, and fines with their types.
- **Session preparation:** an exercise catalogue, a full-page exercise builder and a saved session
  with that exercise.
- **Plans and limits:** free 1 club and 1 team, pro 3 and 5, club 10 and 20, enforced on the server
  rather than only in the interface.

## Data and data model

- Relational model `clubs` → `teams` → `players`.
- `club_matches` → `club_match_players`, with minutes, goals and cards per player and match.
- `club_fines` and `club_fine_types` for fines, and `club_custom_stats` for custom statistics.
- Isolated `demo_*` tables for the public demo, reset daily, with their own authentication.
- PostgreSQL on Supabase with **per-coach RLS** and **transactional RPCs** to save the club; plan
  limits are checked on the server.
- A catalogue of 66 exercises: 26 original and 40 adapted from EBOOK-100.

## What I learned building it

- **Data models and Data Marts:** club → team → player and match → player-match, plus fines and custom
  statistics tables.
- **KPIs and metrics:** minutes, goals and cards per player and match; fines by type; plans and their
  limits.
- **Reports and dashboards:** a club panel with squad, matches, fines and sessions on one screen.
- **Quality and verification:** server-side RLS and RPCs, plan limits checked outside the interface
  and 1,746 unit tests.

## Real state

**Done and verifiable:** a public demo that works end to end, with sign-up and login, plans, a sample
club, squad, matches with minutes, fines, catalogue, exercise builder and saved session. The full
flow passed 10 of 10 checks in a real browser at 390 px; 9 of 9 checks against the real Supabase
backend, including isolation between users; 1,746 unit tests and 7 of 7 end-to-end responsive
management tests.

**Pending:** it is not a production SaaS: there is no real payment gateway (plans are simulated) and
no own SMTP server, and demo data resets every 24 hours. The public multi-user app is ready and
waiting to be deployed. The public catalogue excludes 306 third-party exercises for licensing reasons,
and no minors' or personal data is used.
