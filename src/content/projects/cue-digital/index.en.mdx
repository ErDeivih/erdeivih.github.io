CUE Digital began as a field notebook and is growing into a management tool for farmers. The aim is to bring farm information together without searching through paper records and spreadsheets.

## The field notebook

The application starts with recording activity and farm information. The notebook is the basis for broader management features.

## Farm management

The planned scope includes fields, work, expenses, machinery and contacts. These areas are under development; not all are available yet.

I am also studying information preparation for agricultural subsidies and official exchange formats. That integration still needs to be completed and checked.

## A simpler interface

I am revising the interface to make everyday tasks easier: finding a field, recording work and reviewing completed activity. This is a priority before presenting a broader version.

## Availability

The project is in development, with no public demo linked yet. The first sample will use fictional farm data.
