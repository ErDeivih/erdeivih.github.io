CUE Digital starts with a field notebook and is growing into a management application for farmers. The aim is to keep farm information together instead of spreading it across spreadsheets, paper and different applications.

## Starting point

There is a local application with a Python and FastAPI backend and SQLite storage. Initial work focuses on the notebook and farm information.

## Towards a farm CRM

The planned scope includes land, work, finances, machinery and contacts. These are development areas; not all are available in the current application.

Preparing information for agricultural subsidy processes and official formats also needs further work and checks. The project is not presented as a certified integration or a filing service.

## Next steps

Improving the interface is a priority: making it easier to find a field, record work and review activity. The scope of the first public version is still being defined.

## Status

In local development, with no public demo yet. A future demo will use fictional farm data.
