## Separate documents from their records

The degree project explores keeping encrypted documents and recording versions and permissions through coordinated components.

## Each component's role

- **Ethereum:** contracts record versions, permissions and operations.
- **IPFS Cluster:** stores encrypted content and replicates it between nodes.
- **Client-side encryption:** prepares files before sending them and supports sharing access keys.

## Integrity and availability

The content reference allows checking whether it matches the expected document. Availability depends on the nodes storing that content remaining operational; IPFS alone does not guarantee retention.

The project uses a development network. Its scope and limitations are documented in the [repository](https://github.com/ErDeivih/DocumentChain).
