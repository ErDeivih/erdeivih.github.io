Airport FX reconstructs a common foreign-exchange operator problem: deciding how much cash in each
currency an airport office needs without locking up more money than necessary.

Inventory moves in both directions. Selling foreign currency to somebody leaving Spain reduces that
stock; buying it from somebody arriving replenishes it while consuming euros. Both flows need separate
models before they can be forecast and turned into an inventory decision.

## What makes the project different

There is no public source of individual FX transactions or a complete country-by-airport tourism
table. Instead of hiding that gap, the design distinguishes three layers:

- **Real data**: exchange rates, tourism, spend, passenger and holiday data from public sources.
- **Estimated data**: the country × airport distribution, reconstructed with iterative proportional fitting (IPF).
- **Synthetic data**: simulated transactions that must aggregate back to the official totals.

## The decision it is working towards

On top of that foundation, the roadmap specifies quantile forecasts and a newsvendor policy: select
inventory from the cost of shortage versus idle cash, then validate it with honest temporal backtesting.

## Honest status

This is under development. The Python package, source policy, minimal real fixtures, strict
normalisation, atomic writes, documentation validation and CI exist today. HTTP ingestion, the
dimensional model, Power BI and forecasting are designed but are not presented as complete.
