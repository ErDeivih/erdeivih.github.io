## Sources with provenance

The design uses institutional exchange rates, Spain's FRONTUR and EGATUR datasets, resident travel
statistics, AENA passenger counts and holiday calendars. Every record keeps requested date, effective
date, source and attribution; gaps are never filled without recording how.

## Reconstructing what is not published

The INE publishes tourists by country and destination, while AENA publishes passengers by airport.
The joint matrix is missing. IPF starts with a seed matrix and alternately adjusts rows and columns
until both margins are respected within a declared tolerance.

That does not turn the internal matrix into observed data. It remains an **estimate driven by explicit
assumptions**, and the repository labels it accordingly.

## Simulating in order to validate

Synthetic transactions make it possible to develop the pipeline and models without pretending to
hold company data. The important test is a round trip: once grouped by month, airport, currency and
flow, they must reproduce the aggregates used for calibration.

## Intended architecture

```text
Public sources → partitioned bronze → dbt / dimensional model
                                   ↘ simulator → forecasting → stock policy
```

Dagster orchestrates assets and backfills; DuckDB supports local work; dbt validates transformations;
Power BI is the analytical surface. AWS equivalents are documented, not deployed.
