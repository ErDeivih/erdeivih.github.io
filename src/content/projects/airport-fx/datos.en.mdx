## Planned sources

The design considers exchange rates, tourism and spending statistics, airport passenger figures and calendars. Each data point is intended to retain its source and reporting period.

## Observed and estimated information

Public sources provide aggregate data, but not a complete relationship between origin country, airport and currency exchange transactions.

The approach explores combining aggregates and estimating missing relationships. Estimates will be identified with their assumptions.

## Simulation and analysis

Simulated transactions would support analysis without using real business transactions. Their aggregates would then need to be checked against the source data.

## Planned tools

The design proposes Python and DuckDB for data work, Dagster for collection and transformation, dbt for the model and Power BI for reports. These components are planned; the combined system is not presented as finished.
