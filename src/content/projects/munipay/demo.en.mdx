## Open in the browser

[Open MuniPay](https://multas-c2fed.web.app) to use the web version.

## Review the accounts

An administrator can create a team, add players and record fines, payments and expenses. Players join with an invitation code and check their position.

The summary displays records and outstanding amounts. Excel export lets accounts be reviewed or shared outside the application.

## Android

An [Android version is also available on GitHub](https://github.com/ErDeivih/MuniPay/releases/latest), distributed as an APK.
