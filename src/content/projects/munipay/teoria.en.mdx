## The underlying problem

A team has several money flows mixed together: fines for being late or for a card, material
expenses, and the shared pot. If everyone writes it down wherever, in the end nobody knows how much
is owed or who has paid. MuniPay puts all of that in one place with two clear roles.

## How it's modelled

- **Team** — the group. Created and shared with an invite code.
- **Member / player** — each person in the team, with their role.
- **Fine** — recorded by the admin against a player.
- **Payment** — settling a debt (fine or expense) by a player.
- **Shared expense** — a cost split among members.

## The part I liked most

The relationship between **debt** and **payment**: a player owes an amount, and the app aggregates
what's pending and what's paid without the logic getting complicated. With that, the dashboard and
the export almost come out on their own.
