MuniPay started because keeping track of fines in my football team with a spreadsheet was a mess.
The app creates a team, each player joins with an **invite code**, and from there you record fines,
track who owes and who has paid, and split shared expenses. There's a **dashboard** with what's
happening per team and per player, and you can **export reports to Excel**.

It's published and you can try it: there's a **web version** (no install) and an **Android APK**.

## What it does

- Teams with an invite code and their members.
- Fines and penalties per player.
- Payment and debt tracking.
- Shared team expenses.
- Dashboard with real-time stats.
- Export reports to Excel.
- Two roles: admin and player.

## What I took from it

The whole backend is **Firebase** (Auth + Firestore), no own server, and the UI updates live by
subscribing to the data. The most interesting part was turning that "who owes what" model into a
screen you understand at a glance.
