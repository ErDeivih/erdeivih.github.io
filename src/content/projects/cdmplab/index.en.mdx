CDMPLab is CDM Pizarrales' tactical board. I use it to prepare football drills, save them and put together sessions to share with the coaching staff.

## Design a drill

Place players and equipment, draw movements with arrows, add instructions and draw freehand. Pitch options include a full pitch, half pitch, a third of a pitch, futsal, transverse seven-a-side and a blank canvas.

The board works on desktop, phone and tablet, with controls to move, select and adjust elements.

## Organise training

Save drills in a folder-based library. Search, duplicate and move them to reuse an exercise without starting over.

Sessions combine exercises with timings and can be exported as a training PDF.

## Work with coaching staff

The application includes teams, invitations and permissions for owners and collaborators. Joining a team depends on an invitation and the account's authorisations.

## Access and improvements

[Open CDMPLab in your browser](https://erdeivih.github.io/CDMPLab/). There is nothing to install.

I am improving board interactions and the access process. Opening it to more coaches includes reviewing invitations, account recovery and permissions.
