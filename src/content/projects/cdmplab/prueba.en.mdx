## Open the application

[Visit CDMPLab](https://erdeivih.github.io/CDMPLab/) in your browser on desktop, phone or tablet.

## A board walkthrough

1. Choose a pitch type and create a drill.
2. Place players and equipment; add movements and instructions.
3. Save the drill in the library to retrieve and edit it.
4. Combine drills into a session and add timings.
5. Export the PDF to review or share the training plan.

## Team access

Your account and team membership determine which library and actions are available. Invitations let coaching staff join the team.

[The CDMPLab repository](https://github.com/ErDeivih/CDMPLab) contains the source and development documentation.
