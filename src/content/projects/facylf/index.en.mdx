FACyLF brings together federated football data usually spread across schedules, standings and match reports. It started in Castile and León and has expanded to other regional federations.

The aim is to explore competitions, follow teams and prepare opponent or player reports using the available information.

## Explore federated football

Browse seasons, competitions, matchdays and matches, along with club, team and player profiles. The application includes results, standings and initial performance metrics.

## Collect and maintain data

Collectors retrieve information from each federation. Collection resumes where it stopped and retains original match reports so parsing can be corrected without downloading them again.

Federation data stays separate. Statistics depend on the coverage collected, which is still growing.

## Current work

I am adding seasons and competitions, improving query response times and making reports more useful.

The public version will focus on results and statistics, without player photographs.

## Availability

There is no public access link yet. Preparing deployment and reports is part of the current development work.
