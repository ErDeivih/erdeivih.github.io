FACyLF will be a website for following **federated football in Castile and León** without jumping
between pages or searching for every competition separately.

The idea is to bring results, standings and statistics together in a clear interface, useful both
for checking the latest matchday and following a team's progress through the season.

## What I want to solve

- Find a competition, category or team quickly.
- Check matchdays, results and standings on a phone.
- Present statistics without turning every screen into an endless table.
- Keep navigation consistent across seasons and competitions.

## Before building

The first job is to confirm which data is available, how often it is updated and under which terms
it can be used. From there I can define the competition, team, match and statistics model and choose
an architecture without making the project more complicated than it needs to be.

## Honest status

This is a future project. The repository name is reserved, but the application has not been built yet.
This page records the intention and will grow once there are real decisions and progress to show.
