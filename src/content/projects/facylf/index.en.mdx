FACyLF started with football in Castile and León and has expanded towards other regional federations. It brings together schedules, standings and match reports to explore teams and prepare for matches.

## Available now

The project has collectors for each federation and a local website for competitions, matchdays, results, teams and players. Collection can resume after interruptions; original match reports are retained so parsing can be corrected without downloading them again.

Each federation has separate databases. Initial metrics show available coverage: a profile does not mean a complete playing history has been collected.

## Current work

- Collect more seasons and competitions and check data quality.
- Improve queries so search and profile pages respond faster.
- Make opponent and player reports more useful.
- Prepare a public version with a defined scope.

## No player photographs

The planned public presentation will focus on results and statistics, without player photographs. Portfolio screenshots will follow the same rule.

The application currently runs locally. There is no public demo linked here yet; this page will track application and reporting progress.
