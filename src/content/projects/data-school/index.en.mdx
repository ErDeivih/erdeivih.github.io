Data School has two parts: a study and practice portal for data and AI, and the ordered catalogue of
the content that feeds it. The rule is the same in both: nothing goes in without executed code, a
licence and a test that backs it up.

## The problem

Learning data and AI is easy to start and hard to prove: courses stay in videos and projects stay in
notebooks nobody can open. The material itself was spread across five different origins, with
duplicated content and no shared rule for licences, data or tests.

## What it does

- **Publishes** a portal with 459 sheets, downloadable packages with their own verifier and a
  learning index with hours and prerequisites; search handles synonyms.
- **Orders the catalogue**: 32 checked sources with licence, cost, language and status; 15 only allow
  a link and our own summary, so they are not copied.
- **Publishes its own data**: 10 synthetic datasets under CC0-1.0, with SHA-256 checksum, a fixed-seed
  generator and a quality profile.
- **Checks**: a link checker with a weekly task and decision documents (`ESPECIFICACION.md`,
  `DECISIONES.md`, `DOCTRINA-CALIDAD.md`) recording what enters and what is dropped.

## Data and data model

- **Portal model:** sheets ↔ phases ↔ routes ↔ prerequisites; projects with a package, rubric and
  milestones; packages with verified bytes and `sha256`.
- **13 phases** (`f00`–`f12`), from mathematical foundations to communication and career. Each sheet
  carries frontmatter with `id`, `titulo`, `fase`, `nivel`, `rutas`, `prerequisitos`, `tags`,
  `resumen` and `fuentes`.
- **Figures with provenance:** 350 Python scripts generate 370 SVGs and each figure points to its
  source.
- **Entry rule:** every concept with executed code; every dataset with a licence and provenance.
- **Automated gates:** checks for usability, spacing, library, downloads and offline use; ZIP
  validation against the manifest.
- **Data quality:** 10/10 CSVs load, 63/63 columns and types correct, 0 nulls and 0 duplicates; 82
  links checked (79 live, 3 redirect, 0 dead).

## What it shows for a Business Analyst role

- **Data models and Data Marts:** the sheet–phase–route–prerequisite relationship, packages with
  `sha256` and datasets with a sheet and a quality profile.
- **ETL:** Markdown and YAML turned into semantic HTML, a search index, a glossary, datasets and an
  offline manifest.
- **KPIs and coverage:** coverage measured per phase and per dataset, with a count of checks in every
  automated gate.
- **Quality and debugging:** ZIP validation against the manifest, 0 nulls and 0 duplicates in the
  CSVs, a link checker and a documented correction of a wrong figure (the "link-only" sources are
  15, not 11).

## Real state

**Done and verifiable:** portal published on GitHub Pages with the index, search and downloadable
packages; catalogue consolidated and measured from five origins; automated gates green. No GitHub
Actions: everything runs locally.

**Pending:** 9 legal fields for the site owner that the portal itself marks as missing, a dark theme
in progress, ten project sheets still to complete and a real usage metric (downloads and sessions) to
turn coverage into impact.
