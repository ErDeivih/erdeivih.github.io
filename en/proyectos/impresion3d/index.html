Pre-launch site for a framed relief piece with the coats of arms of four surnames: a physical product sold with a clear price and a proof before printing.

The problem

A family heraldry commission is decided by two questions: how much it costs and how I know it will look right. The site answers both before the customer leaves their details: closed price and proof before printing.

What it does

  • Configurator: four coats of arms, sizes and frames with pricing rules; the price is calculated on the spot.
  • Three pieces: four-coat frame (€189 VAT included), helmet with mantling and medallion, each with its size and price.
  • Request without payment: the information form charges nothing and books no order; the deposit (€56.70) is only paid when designing.
  • Proof first: before printing, the proof is sent and production only continues once the customer approves it.

Data and data model

The catalogue (coats, sizes, frames and configurator rules) and the public offer live in JSON data; the site is static and published on GitHub Pages. The price is derived from the offer, not typed by hand on each page.

What it shows for a Business Analyst role

  • Product and pricing: an offer with a closed price, deposit and remainder, explained without small print.
  • Business rules modelled: the configurator turns coats, size and frame into a price.
  • Operations: the pre-launch site captures requests without charging and without promising a booking.

Real status

Done and verifiable: public demo with catalogue, configurator, prices and an information request that charges nothing; the main piece’s price is closed (€189 VAT included).

Pending: the commercial brand is not decided yet (the demo header still carries a brand placeholder), final legal texts and the real payment gateway.
