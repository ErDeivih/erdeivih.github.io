CDMPLab

Plan a training session and keep the drills ready to use again.

The key idea:Available on the web, with a tactical board, exercise library and sessions.

  • Angular
  • TypeScript
  • SVG
  • Supabase
Open application ↗Code on GitHub ↗

CDMPLab is CDM Pizarrales’ tactical board. I use it to prepare football drills, save them and put together sessions to share with the coaching staff.

Design a drill

Place players and equipment, draw movements with arrows, add instructions and draw freehand. Pitch options include a full pitch, half pitch, a third of a pitch, futsal, transverse seven-a-side and a blank canvas.

The board works on desktop, phone and tablet, with controls to move, select and adjust elements.

Organise training

Save drills in a folder-based library. Search, duplicate and move them to reuse an exercise without starting over.

Sessions combine exercises with timings and can be exported as a training PDF.

Work with coaching staff

The application includes teams, invitations and permissions for owners and collaborators. Joining a team depends on an invitation and the account’s authorisations.

Access and improvements

Open CDMPLab in your browser. There is nothing to install.

I am improving board interactions and the access process. Opening it to more coaches includes reviewing invitations, account recovery and permissions.

The key idea

Available on the web, with a tactical board, exercise library and sessions.

An editable board

A drill is saved as structured elements: pitch, players, equipment, positions, arrows and text. This lets it be reopened and edited instead of keeping only an image.

The pitch uses SVG. The interface is built with Angular and TypeScript.

Library and sessions

Folders organise drills in the library. Sessions combine tasks and timings, with PDF output for taking a prepared plan to training.

Teams and permissions

Accounts and shared data use Supabase. Permissions are checked on the server and distinguish team management from exercise editing.

The repository documents storage, access and collaboration decisions.

Open the application

Visit CDMPLab in your browser on desktop, phone or tablet.

A board walkthrough

  1. Choose a pitch type and create a drill.
  2. Place players and equipment; add movements and instructions.
  3. Save the drill in the library to retrieve and edit it.
  4. Combine drills into a session and add timings.
  5. Export the PDF to review or share the training plan.

Team access

Your account and team membership determine which library and actions are available. Invitations let coaching staff join the team.

Code and documentation

The CDMPLab repository contains the source and development documentation.
