CUE Digital

Record what the law requires in a farm field notebook and refuse to save an illegal entry.

CUE Digital is a farm’s field notebook. It records what the regulation requires, blocks an illegal entry and explains in plain Spanish what is missing before an inspection arrives.

What it is, what it is for and what I built

What it is. A digital field notebook for a farm: mandatory records, validations and traceability.

What it is for. To record what the law requires without being able to save an illegal entry, and to keep plots, field work and invoices in the same place.

What I built. The farm data model, the validations that block an incorrect entry, plot import from the CAP geographic system, invoices and the public demo with made-up data.

What I did not do. The official submission to the regional government (IUWS) is implemented and tested against the annex, but it has not been connected in production. Demo data is made up; a real client’s data is not published.

The problem

The digital field notebook stops being optional: fertiliser records from 1 January 2026, a fertiliser plan from 1 September 2026, electronic records of applied products from 1 January 2027 and the end of paper records on 31 December 2026.

The app is designed for a farmer with no IT background. A mistake in the dose, in the applicator’s licence or in the waiting days becomes a penalty, and an unsigned notebook proves nothing in an inspection.

Approach

  • Mandatory records: treatments (product, dose, area, pest, justification, applicator, machine and weather conditions), fertilisers with pure-nutrient calculation, field work, irrigation and harvest.
  • Blocks illegal entries and explains what to do: product without a label number or withdrawn, dose above the label, more hectares than the plot, expired applicator licence, expired machine inspection, harvesting before the waiting days or a product not allowed in organic farming.
  • Warns about expiries before it is too late: licence, machine inspection and containers.
  • A PDF paper notebook that is produced even with missing data, with a final sheet of what is missing, and a CSV export ready for Excel.
  • Notebook signature with AutoFirma and the fingerprint recalculated on every request.
  • Plots from SIGPAC and from the regional government’s notebook, without typing references, plus invoice reading from PDF or photo.

Data and data model

  • Scope and isolation: the farm identifier is on every business table, which is what isolates one farmer from another.
  • Nothing is deleted: entries are cancelled with a reason and a trace remains in the audit table.
  • Frozen history: label data is copied into the entry, so correcting a product sheet never changes a historical entry.
  • Geometry as WKT text, so the same code works on SQLite and PostgreSQL.
  • Legal calculations isolated with their own tests: waiting days, pure nutrient, doses and validity periods.
  • Separate texts, with a test that checks no jargon a farmer would not say has slipped in.
  • Official sources with a fingerprint: the government catalogues and interfaces are stored with their SHA-256 so it can be proven they are the official ones.

What I learned building it

  • Data models and Data Marts: a per-farm relational model with isolation, audit and frozen history.
  • Data quality and debugging: validations that block illegal entries, plain-language messages and tolerance for what is unknown, without invented negative answers.
  • Regulatory requirements and traceability: obligations cited article by article with a BOE link, and tests that check the dates do not go stale.
  • Systems integration: SIGPAC, the regional notebook, the administration interface, AutoFirma and CAdES, all with an official source and fingerprint.
  • KPIs and metrics: notebook completeness calculated from the data (what is missing and why), not typed by hand.

Real state

Done and verifiable: mandatory records, validations, signature with fingerprint and checked CAdES, plots from SIGPAC and the regional notebook, invoices and the official interface client. The public demo was verified on 2026-11-10 with five plots in Salamanca and an always-visible demo notice; data typed there stays in the browser. The full app runs locally with SQLite by default and PostgreSQL documented. 6,116 Python tests and 1,322 JavaScript tests in the project suite.

Pending: the import JSON is not sent to the government yet: the catalogue values, access to the integration environment and the domain certificate are missing. The CAdES trust chain is not checked, the app sends no email and the expansion into full farm management is still in development. Tests with real farmers are still needed. There is no public repository: the cited remote returns 404.
