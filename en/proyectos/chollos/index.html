Chollos

Sort genuine second-hand bargains from the noise by comparing each price with the median of its comparables.

The key idea:Every pass publishes downloads, rule-based discards, valued listings and savings, with each listing's history.

  • Python
  • FastAPI
  • SQLite
  • Pydantic
  • selectolax

Chollos is a personal second-hand search tool. It collects listings from four verticals, discards noise before scoring, compares each price with the median of its comparables and leaves a short list with its detail page. It does not buy: it informs and lets the user decide.

The problem

On second-hand marketplaces, most of what appears is not a bargain: accessories, spare parts, duplicated listings, month-old prices or outright scams. The good ones get lost among hundreds of results, and no platform compares the price with the market or warns whether a listing is fresh.

What it does

  1. Collects listings from four verticals: motorcycles around €500, cars under €2,000, PC components and mini PCs.
  2. Discards noise before scoring: accessories, parts and duplicated listings.
  3. Compares the price with the median of comparables (same product, family and generation), with a capped discount to catch impossible prices and a confidence level.
  4. Checks seller risk and listing freshness.
  5. Publishes a short list with each listing’s page, history and lowest-price mark, a daily summary file and a CSV export.
  6. Runs as a hidden daily Windows task, with a readable log and no notifications.

Data and data model

  • Sources: 12 active source definitions, respecting robots.txt and per-domain pacing; extraction from HTML, embedded JSON and APIs, normalised into a shared contract and deduplicated.
  • Store: SQLite with listings, prices, valuations and runs, plus compressed raw data so it can be reprocessed without downloading again.
  • Data contract and criteria: ALCANCE-COMPRA.md defines what a bargain is, DATOS.md fixes the contract and DOCTRINA-CALIDAD.md defines what “finished” means.
  • Per-pass metrics in metricas-chollos.json: downloads, discards by rule, valued listings, AI usage, verdicts and savings.
  • Per-listing history: a series of observations, the lowest price seen with its date and a return-to-low warning.

What it shows for a Business Analyst role

  • KPIs and metrics: downloads, discards by rule, valued listings, verdicts and savings on every pass, with history.
  • Data quality and debugging: deduplication, rule-based discards before scoring, a data contract and a quality doctrine.
  • ETL: 12 sources, HTML, JSON and API extraction, normalisation into a shared contract and raw data kept for reprocessing.
  • Data models and Data Marts: per-listing observation history, minima and a valuation table.
  • Reports and dashboards: a panel with per-vertical counters, a weekly view, a listing page with history and CSV export.
  • Price analysis: a median-of-comparables reference, a capped discount and a confidence level.

Real state

Done and verifiable: a local panel that works end to end, with fresh data, CSV export, an automatic daily summary and a scheduled daily task. A measured full pass: 4,779 listings seen, 2,321 new and 10,040 valued, with €264 of estimated savings in 28 minutes. The flow was verified on 2026-11-10 (panel, “best real discount” filter, listing page with history and CSV) and 325 tests are green.

Pending: there is no public demo and no public repository (access on request): the demo is local and this page relies on screenshots and figures. Two active Milanuncios links return 403 to the automated client and are deliberately kept, because an interruption does not prove the listing is gone. Comparing the lowest price across listings of the same model and finishing the eBay production integration remain open.

The number of listings seen compared with the few active ones is the filter working as intended, not a growth metric.
