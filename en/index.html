About

About me

I am a computer engineer studying the Master's in Computer Engineering at the University of Salamanca. I like building software that actually works: applications, services and the system pieces behind them.

I code mainly in Java, TypeScript, C# and Python, and I have worked with Node.js, React, Spring, PostgreSQL, Docker and Git. In the projects in this portfolio I have built the data collection, the model, the backend, the frontend and the deployment; some are published and others only run locally, and each page says which. I do data analysis as a way of learning, not as a speciality.
