Profile

A little about me

I studied Computer Engineering at the University of Salamanca and I am focusing my profile on data and BI: understanding a process, organising its data and turning it into information that can be consulted and used to decide.

I am looking for my first Business Analyst internship. To prepare, I work on personal projects that include the core pieces of the job: data sources, data models, loading processes, KPIs and reports. Some can already be tried; others still run locally. The project pages describe each one's real status and what is missing.

01

Understand the process before modelling

Before choosing tables or metrics, I try to describe what a person decides and which data they need for it. MuniPay started from an awkward spreadsheet; Fondo Popular from data scattered across federations.

02

Write decisions down

Each page explains where the data comes from, how it relates and what the result's limits are. It does not just list technologies.

03

Be clear about unfinished work

If something is a prototype or not deployed yet, I say so: the real status says more than claiming more than exists.
